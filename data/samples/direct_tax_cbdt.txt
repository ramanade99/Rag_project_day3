Section 1. TDS on Contractor Payments
The Income Tax Act prescribes under Section 194C that a payer must deduct TDS at 1 percent on payments to individual contractors and at 2 percent on payments to other contractors. The CBDT notifies the threshold of Rs. 30,000 for a single payment.

Section 2. Retrospective Clarification
The CBDT clarified through a circular that the definition of work in Section 194C covers manufacturing contracts. This is a retrospective clarification and not a prospective amendment of the Income Tax Act.

Section 3. Failure to Deduct
Where the Tax Authority finds that TDS was not deducted, Section 201 treats the payer as an assessee in default and the Income Tax Dept levies interest at 1 percent per month.
