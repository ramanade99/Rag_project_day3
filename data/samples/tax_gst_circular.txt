Section 1. E-Invoicing under GST

Section 2. Input Service Distributor
Under the CGST Act, an ISD must distribute input tax credit on common input services to its recipient units. The GST Council clarified that ISD registration is mandatory from the effective date of the amendment; this clarification applies prospectively and not retrospectively.

Section 3. Late Reporting Penalty
